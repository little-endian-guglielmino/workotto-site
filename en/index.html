A sheet built the way you think

Build up to five days, each with its own colour, and fill them with blocks: straight sets, supersets, circuits and dropsets. Set sets, reps, RIR and rest for every exercise.

  • Drag to reorder, duplicate or delete days and sheets
  • Over 1,300 exercises, searchable by name, muscle or equipment

BTrain

Made for the gym floor, not the demo

Big buttons, a built-in number keypad and last session's values right where you type the new ones. Tick a set and the rest timer starts on its own.

  • Automatic rest timer with haptic cues in the last seconds
  • Screen stays awake during the session
  • One tap to confirm “same as last time”

CTrack

See whether you are really improving

Max load and estimated 1RM over time for every exercise, personal records highlighted, weekly volume per muscle group and a history of every session with notes and body weight.

  • No invented scores, no goals you never set

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts: you create a local profile with your name and unit and you can start.

Does it work without internet?

Always. No feature needs a connection: workouts, history and charts all live on your device.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. If you change phones, export your sheets as files and import them on the new device.

Is it free?

Yes, downloading and using it is free.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, with Android phones coming too.

Can I use pounds instead of kilograms?

Yes, pick kg or lb in settings. Your data keeps its precision when you switch.
