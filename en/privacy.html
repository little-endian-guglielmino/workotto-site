Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

In short

Workotto does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What data the app handles

Sheets, workouts, custom exercises, notes, body weight, your profile name and, if you choose one, your profile photo. This data is stored only in your device's local storage and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Third-party services

The app uses no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting tools and no other service that talks to outside servers.

Sharing

If you export a sheet, the file is shared only with the app or person you choose through the system share sheet.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app, which erases it. Because there is no account and no copy on our servers, we cannot recover it.

Children

The app collects no data of any kind, including from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.
